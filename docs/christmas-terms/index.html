1. Bookings and Payment

All bookings must be made through the official SHE booking page. A place is confirmed once full payment has been received and a booking confirmation has been issued.

Tickets are valid for one person only unless otherwise stated.

2. Cancellations, Refunds and Ticket Transfers

Due to the nature of event planning and advance commitments to venues and suppliers, all customer bookings are considered final and tickets are non-refundable if you are unable to attend, except where a refund is required by law.

However, if you can no longer attend, you are welcome to transfer your ticket to a friend or another person. Please notify SHE in advance with the name of the replacement attendee so we can update our guest list.

If SHE has to cancel the event, ticket holders will be contacted and offered a refund of their ticket price.

Nothing in these terms affects your statutory consumer rights.

3. Event Changes

SHE reserves the right to make reasonable changes to the event arrangements where necessary, including timings, catering, activities or other operational details.

If a significant change or cancellation occurs, attendees will be notified using the contact details provided at booking.

4. Dietary Requirements and Allergies

Please notify us of any dietary requirements, intolerances or allergies when booking.

We will communicate these requirements to the venue and catering team wherever possible. However, we cannot guarantee an entirely allergen-free environment or prevent cross-contamination.

Guests with serious allergies should contact us before booking to discuss their requirements.

5. Drinks and Refreshments

Your Christmas Gathering ticket includes the drinks and refreshments specified in the event listing.

Any additional drinks not included in your ticket can be purchased separately from the venue.

Please note that any additional purchases are the responsibility of the attendee.

6. Charity Raffle

Our Christmas raffle will support a nominated local charity.

100% of the money raised through raffle ticket sales will go towards the nominated charity.

We are incredibly grateful to the local businesses and individuals who have generously donated prizes.

Please note that raffle tickets must be purchased in advance through the designated booking link. Raffle tickets will not be available to purchase at the event.

We encourage guests who wish to participate to purchase their raffle tickets before attending the gathering.

7. Photography and Filming

Photography and filming may take place during the event for SHE’s marketing and promotional purposes.

If you would prefer not to appear in photographs or videos, please let a member of the SHE team know when you arrive. We will respect your wishes and take reasonable steps to ensure you are not included in promotional photography or filming.

8. Guest Behaviour and Safety

We want everyone to feel welcome, comfortable and safe.

All attendees are expected to treat fellow guests, venue staff and organisers with kindness and respect.

SHE reserves the right to ask anyone behaving inappropriately or creating an unsafe environment to leave the event, with no refund where legally permitted.

9. Personal Belongings and Responsibility

Attendees are responsible for their own personal belongings throughout the event.

SHE is not responsible for loss, theft or damage to personal property, except where liability cannot legally be excluded.

10. Arrival and Attendance

Please arrive in good time for the advertised start time.

If you arrive late, we will do our best to accommodate you without disrupting the experience for other guests.

11. Acceptance of Terms

By purchasing a ticket and completing the required acceptance question at checkout, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Terms & Conditions.

We look forward to welcoming you to SHE.
