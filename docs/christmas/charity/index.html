Our chosen charity

Supporting BENS Groups

At SHE, we believe that everyone deserves to feel seen, supported, accepted and valued. That’s why we’re incredibly proud to be supporting BENS Groups through our Christmas fundraising raffle.

We believe children are our future. And now, more than ever, it’s important that we protect, nurture and champion the next generation, particularly those who see the world a little differently.

The children who are creative, curious, imaginative and think outside the box. The children who might not always fit into traditional ways of learning or communicating. The ones who may have been made to feel they’re not clever enough or somehow different, when in reality they’re incredibly bright, capable and extraordinary individuals with so much to offer the world.

Every child deserves someone who sees their potential, believes in them and gives them the space to discover who they are.

This is something BENS Groups does beautifully.

BENS Groups is a wonderful local community organisation dedicated to supporting children and young people, creating safe, nurturing and non judgemental spaces where they can feel understood, accepted and free to be themselves.

Through their after school sessions, children are given the opportunity to explore their emotions, build confidence and self esteem, develop friendships and learn valuable tools to help them navigate anxiety, communication and everyday challenges.

Using creative activities, games, arts and crafts and gentle coaching, BENS Groups helps children develop a better understanding of themselves and the world around them.

But perhaps most importantly, it gives them something every child deserves: a sense of belonging.

Their support doesn’t stop with the children. BENS Groups also works closely with parents and families, offering a listening ear, practical guidance and support with things such as EHCPs and school advocacy.

Because supporting a child often means supporting the whole family. Having someone in your corner who listens, understands and helps you navigate the challenges can make an enormous difference.

Why we’re fundraising

Like many small community organisations, BENS Groups is facing the increasing pressure of rising running costs.

Despite this, their commitment remains unwavering: to keep their after school sessions running at a minimal cost, ensuring that children can access the support they need regardless of their family’s financial circumstances.

We want to help BENS Groups continue the incredible work they do within our local community.

100% of the money raised through our Christmas raffle will go towards supporting BENS Groups and their essential running costs.

Every raffle ticket purchased will help make a difference to local children and families.
