Supporting a cause close to our hearts

SHE Christmas is more than a beautiful evening together, it's also an opportunity to give back.

This year, our Christmas raffle will support a nominated local charity that makes a real difference in our community.

  • The Christmas raffle

    The raffle is how SHE Christmas gives back. It will raise funds for our nominated charity.

  • Prizes from our community

    Local businesses and individuals are warmly invited to contribute raffle prizes.

  • How you can take part

    On the night, guests will be able to buy raffle tickets, and may also make an additional voluntary contribution if they wish. It is entirely optional.

To be clear: the fundraising comes from the raffle and from any optional contributions guests choose to make. It is not taken from ticket sales.

“It's about coming together, not just to celebrate, but to support and uplift our community.”
