Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining us for a special evening of connection, celebration and Christmas magic.

Bookings & Tickets

How do I book my place?

You can book your place through our online booking page. Simply select your ticket, complete your payment and follow the booking instructions. We can’t wait to welcome you!

Is my ticket confirmed once payment has been made?

Yes! Once your booking and payment have been successfully completed, your place is secured. You’ll receive a booking confirmation by email, so please keep this for your records.

Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely! We’d love you to bring a friend. Just remember that everyone attending needs their own ticket, and bookings are subject to availability.

Can I purchase additional tickets after booking?

Of course, subject to availability. Simply return to the booking page and purchase any additional tickets available.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Yes! If you’re unable to attend, you’re welcome to transfer your ticket to someone else. Simply notify SHE in advance with the new attendee’s full name and email address so we can update our guest list.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable if you change your mind or are no longer able to attend. However, you are welcome to transfer your ticket to another person by notifying us in advance. If SHE has to cancel the event, all ticket holders will receive a full refund.

Cancellations & Changes

What happens if I can no longer attend?

We understand that plans can change. Although tickets are non-refundable, you are welcome to transfer your place to someone else. Just contact us with their full name and email address so we can update the booking.

What is the cancellation deadline?

As tickets are non-refundable, there is no cancellation deadline. If you can no longer attend, please let us know as soon as possible so we can update our guest list.

Are there any cancellation or administration fees?

No. We don’t charge any additional administration or cancellation fees.

What happens if SHE has to cancel the event?

If SHE needs to cancel the Christmas Gathering, all guests who have purchased tickets will receive a full refund.

What happens if the event date or venue changes?

If there are any significant changes to the event date or venue, we’ll contact all registered guests directly with the updated information and explain the options available.

What happens if someone drops out? Will their ticket become available again?

If someone can no longer attend, they can transfer their ticket to another person by notifying us of the new guest’s full name and email address. Any remaining availability will be reflected on our booking page.

Food & Dietary Requirements

Are food and refreshments included in my ticket?

Yes! Your ticket includes a welcome drink (our SHE Sparkle), a festive buffet, a Christmas sweet treat and a warming hot chocolate.

Can I let you know about dietary requirements or allergies?

Absolutely! If you have any dietary requirements or allergies, please reply directly to your booking confirmation email from hello@sheishome.co.uk. This allows us to speak with our catering team in advance and make the necessary arrangements.

Can vegetarian, vegan or other dietary options be accommodated?

Please let us know your requirements by replying directly to your booking confirmation email from hello@sheishome.co.uk. We’ll discuss these with our catering team and do our best to accommodate them.

Who should I contact if I have a food allergy?

Please reply directly to your booking confirmation email from hello@sheishome.co.uk, including details of your allergy. If you have a serious allergy or specific concerns about ingredients or cross-contamination, please contact us as soon as possible so we can discuss this with our catering team.

Location & Arrival

Where is the event taking place?

We’ll be meeting at The Moat House Inn, near Alcester, in their beautiful Christmas Teepee.

We’ll send registered guests a further email closer to the event with more detailed arrival information.

Is there parking available?

Yes! There’s plenty of parking available on site, so you won’t need to worry about finding somewhere to leave your car.

Is parking free?

Yes, parking is available free of charge at the venue.

What time should I arrive?

The gathering runs from 7:30pm until 10:30pm. We’d recommend arriving in good time so you can settle in, grab your welcome drink and enjoy the evening from the start.

Will there be signs to help me find the Teepee?

We’ll share relevant arrival instructions with guests before the event to help you find us easily. Keep an eye on your inbox for our final reminder!

Is the venue accessible?

Our event is intended to be accessible and welcoming to everyone. If you have any particular accessibility requirements or would like to discuss anything before attending, please get in touch. We’ll be happy to help.

Drinks & Refreshments

What drinks are included in my ticket?

Your ticket includes our welcome drink, the SHE Sparkle, as well as a warming hot chocolate.

Can I purchase additional drinks?

Absolutely! Additional drinks will be available to purchase separately from the venue’s bar throughout the evening.

Can I bring my own drinks?

Please contact us before bringing any drinks to the event. Additional drinks will be available to purchase directly from the venue.

The Charity Raffle

How does the charity raffle work?

We’ll be hosting a special Christmas raffle featuring lovely prizes generously donated by local businesses and individuals. Raffle tickets must be bought in advance through our website, and every ticket supports BENS Groups, our nominated local charity.

Where does the money raised go?

Our Christmas raffle supports BENS Groups, a local community organisation for children and families. You can read more about them on our Giving Back page.

Can I purchase raffle tickets on the evening?

Raffle tickets must be purchased in advance using the link on our website or in the welcome email. Unfortunately, they won’t be available to purchase on the evening itself.

Can I buy raffle tickets without attending the event?

The raffle is exclusively for guests attending the SHE Christmas Gathering, so you must be present to participate. However, if you’d still like to support our nominated charity but can’t attend, charitable donations are always welcome.

The Experience

What should I wear?

Whatever makes you feel comfortable! There’s absolutely no dress code. You can dress up, keep it casual or add a little festive sparkle if you fancy it. The most important thing is that you feel like yourself.

Do I need to come with someone?

Not at all! You’re very welcome to come on your own. In fact, many of our experiences are designed to bring people together and create new connections.

What if I don’t know anybody?

That’s absolutely fine. You won’t be the only person stepping into a room of new faces. SHE is all about creating a welcoming space where women can meet, connect and feel comfortable being themselves.

Will there be opportunities to meet new people?

Absolutely! Connection is at the heart of the SHE experience. There’ll be opportunities to chat, enjoy some games and surprises, share stories and hopefully leave with a few new connections.

Is there anything I need to bring?

Just yourself, an open mind and an open heart! We’ve taken care of the little details, including your festive treats and a little gift from SHE. Come ready to relax, connect and enjoy the evening.

Still have a question?

We’d love to hear from you!

If there’s anything we’ve not covered, or you’d simply like to know a little more before booking, please don’t hesitate to get in touch.

You can email us at hello@sheishome.co.uk and we’ll be more than happy to help.

We can’t wait to welcome you!
