A little something
different this Christmas...

Christmas can be a wonderfully busy time, but it's easy to forget to make a little time for ourselves.

SHE Christmas is an invitation to do just that.

We're bringing women together for a festive evening filled with warmth, connection, good company and a little Christmas magic.

The setting

The Teepee

Join us at The Moat House Inn, Nr Alcester, where their beautiful teepee creates a cosy and intimate space for an evening of connection and celebration.
