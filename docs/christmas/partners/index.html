Her offering
Crystals, books, rituals and soulful gifts.

“To share tools and rituals that help people feel more grounded, inspired and at home within themselves.”

Abi

Abi Jam Yoga

Her story
Abi is passionate about sharing accessible yoga with people from all walks of life and has 400+ hours of yoga training including spending time in an Indian Ashram.
Her offering

Megan Hancox

Allura Crystal

Her story
Allura Crystal was created as a space for moments of self care and wellbeing, as a reminder for herself and for others.Her inspiration comes from the beautiful Cotswolds, where she grew up and goes back to when she wants to slow down and centre herself again. It also comes from her children, nature, slow living and her background in crystal therapy and holistic treatments.

Raffle contribution

A bespoke crystal bracelet, so that someone can choose what they really need

Emily White

Integrated Healing

@harmonisedbyemily

Her story
Emily is a trained Integrated Healing practitioner, having completed her practitioner training while living in Dubai.Her own experience with Integrated Healing sparked a passion for understanding the connection between the mind, body and subconscious. After experiencing some powerful personal breakthroughs, she felt inspired to learn the modality herself and share it with others.
Her offering
Emily offers one to one Integrated Healing Balance sessions, combining kinesiology (muscle testing), energy work, subconscious reprogramming, emotional release and belief work.Using muscle testing as a guide, sessions aim to uncover underlying emotional, mental and energetic blocks, helping clients explore patterns and beliefs that may be holding them back.Every session is individual, working with what comes up on the day.
Her why
Emily believes that sometimes the things holding us back aren't always obvious. Our subconscious beliefs, past experiences and emotional patterns can influence how we feel, think and show up in everyday life.Having experienced the benefits of Integrated Healing herself, she is passionate about helping others reconnect with themselves, release what no longer serves them and feel lighter, clearer and more aligned.Her philosophy is simple: sometimes we need to look a little deeper to understand what our mind and body are trying to tell us.

Rhiannon

Body and Soul Therapies

Her story
Rhiannon is passionate about helping people take time out of their busy lives to reconnect with themselves through the power of touch and therapeutic massage.Through Body and Soul Therapies, she creates a welcoming space where people can slow down, switch off and prioritise their own wellbeing.
Her offering
Rhiannon is a massage therapist offering treatments designed to encourage relaxation, ease physical tension and support overall wellbeing.Her approach focuses on creating a personalised experience, recognising that everybody's body and needs are different.
Her why
Rhiannon believes that looking after ourselves shouldn't be a luxury or an afterthought.She understands the importance of taking time to nurture both body and mind, allowing ourselves the space to rest, recharge and feel good again.Through Body and Soul Therapies, she hopes to help people make self care a regular part of their lives rather than something they only turn to when they're running on empty.
