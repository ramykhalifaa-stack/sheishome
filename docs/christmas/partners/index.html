SHE Christmas

The peopleBehind the Magic

Meet the incredible women and independent businesses making SHE Christmas extra special.

A Community
of Women

SHE is about bringing people together, and we're so proud to be supported by a collection of wonderful, local, women-led businesses.

Each of these amazing women is sharing their time, talent and creativity to help make this Christmas gathering a truly special experience.

Natalie

The Happy Witch

Her story
Natalie created The Happy Witch as a space for people to explore, heal and feel a little more connected to themselves.
Her offering
Crystals, books, rituals and soulful gifts.
Her why
“To share tools and rituals that help people feel more grounded, inspired and at home within themselves.”

Raffle contribution

A curated crystal gift set

Emma

Rooted Retreats

Her story
Emma runs nurturing retreats that bring together movement, nourishment and nature, creating space for women to breathe, reset and feel good again.
Her offering
Wellbeing experiences, yoga and mindfulness.
Her why
“To give women the chance to slow down, reconnect and remember how good it feels to look after themselves.”

Raffle contribution

A private yoga session for two

Laura

Laura James Styling

Her story
Laura helps women simplify their style, build wardrobes they love and feel like the best version of themselves.
Her offering
Personal styling, wardrobe edits and style guides.
Her why
“Because when you feel good in what you wear, it changes how you show up in every area of your life.”

Raffle contribution

A styling session and style guide
