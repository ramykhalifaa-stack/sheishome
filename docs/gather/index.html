Coffee Conversations

A different kind of coffee morning.

Good coffee. Better questions. Conversations worth having.

A small table. A handful of carefully chosen questions. And a space where you don't have to know anyone before you arrive.

Come on your own or bring someone you know.

You don't need to know anyone.
You just need to be open.

Find your coffee conversation
What it is

A space to meet andhave better conversations.

We all know how easy it is to fall into the usual small talk. SHE Coffee Conversations is a simple way to get beyond that and have conversations that feel more interesting, more meaningful and, at times, completely unexpected.

Some will make you laugh. Some will make you think. And sometimes, you might leave having met someone who feels like a friend you just hadn't met yet.

There's no pressure to share more than you want to. Just come, sit down and see where the conversation takes you.

How it works

01

Take a seat

Come alone or bring someone you know. You'll join a small table and settle in with your coffee, tea and something sweet.

02

Pick a question

Choose the one that makes you lean in.

03

Start talking

The questions give you somewhere to start. From there, let the conversation go where it naturally wants to.

04

See where it goes

You might discover something new about yourself, have a conversation you weren't expecting, or simply enjoy a really lovely morning.

The questions

Options for where theconversation takes you.

A carefully curated selection of questions gives you options, from light and easy to more curious and thought-provoking.

Not every question is for everyone, and that's the point. You choose what feels right in the moment.

The idea isn't to force connection. It's simply to give it a chance to happen.

Your SHE Coffee Notebook

A notebook forwhat stays with you.

Your first Coffee Conversation includes a SHE Coffee Notebook.

A place to capture the thoughts, ideas and moments you want to remember.

Inside, you'll also find your SHE Coffee Journey, a record of the conversations you've been part of and the places you've shared them.
