SHE + ME

Dream catcher Workshop

Two experiences. One shared moment.

  • 16 January 2027
  • Location not confirmed
  • Suitable for ages 4+

What if you could give yourself a little time to dream, while creating a magical memory together?

SHE + ME is a creative experience designed for two generations to enjoy both a little time for themselves and meaningful time together.

Book Your Place

A little time for SHE.A little time for ME.

Because connection starts with having the space to be yourself, too.

The SHE experience

A moment to pause, reflect and reconnect with the things that matter to you.

You’ll spend time exploring your own manifestation, setting intentions and giving some thought to the dreams and possibilities you’d love to bring into your next chapter.

The ME experience

While SHE takes her moment, ME has their own creative space.

With age-appropriate activities such as drawing, colouring, creative journalling and other little projects, they’ll have the freedom to explore their imagination and express themselves.

Suitable for children aged 4+.

Then, it’s timeto come together.

The shared experience

Once you’ve both enjoyed your individual activities, it’s time to reconnect.

Together, you’ll create your very own dream catcher.

A chance to share ideas, get creative, work together and make something beautiful that you can take home.

Not just something you’ve made, but something that reminds you of the time you spent together.

A little morethan an afternoon.

SHE + ME is about celebrating the relationship between two people, creating space for individuality and making memories along the way.

Whether it’s mum and daughter, grandma and granddaughter, auntie and niece, or another special pairing, everyone deserves a little time together.

Come as you are. Create separately. Reconnect together.
