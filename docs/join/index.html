What happens when you join
01

You'll hear from us.

When there's something worth sharing: a new conversation, a question we're sitting with, a gathering, a retreat, something we've made.

02

You'll find your way in.

Follow the chapter you're living. Explore something that catches you. Come to something in real life. Pick up a journal. Or just have a look around.

03

You might find your people.

Women who get it. Because that's really the point.
