Why it exists

It started witha question.

The Becoming began as a personal tool, a way to make sense of my own life, my thoughts and what I wanted next.

It quickly became so much more. A space for honest reflection, deeper questions and real change.

This journal is here to support you through the in-between, the unknown and the becoming.
