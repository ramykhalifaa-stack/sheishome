SHE
Sacred Human Experience

What started with a journal became an experience.
The experience became gatherings, conversations, retreats, questions, journals and everything that SHE continues to grow into.

I was trying to understand my own life. How I was spending my days, how I was feeling, what was affecting my energy and what I wanted to change.

I started making myself a plan.

I broke my days down, paid attention to my cycle and began noticing the relationship between what was happening in my life and how I was feeling.

Then I turned it into a journal.

I shared it with a friend, and the idea grew.

I started imagining something bigger around it, bringing together reflection, photography, integrated healing and the Power Journal to create an experience that women could actually step into.

Same questions.
Brighter horizons.

The people.

Because SHE was never only about becoming a better version of yourself.

It was about the people that you meet along the way as well. The women who make life feel a little lighter, a little deeper, a little more you.

The friends who hold space, who challenge you with love, who inspire you, who see you, who remind you what matters, and who are simply good to be around.

The kind of women you can talk to for hours, go away with, laugh with, learn from, and come home feeling like you've found something real.

That's the part we didn't plan for.
And perhaps the part that matters most.
