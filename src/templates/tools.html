The story{{ lines("Two different moments.\nThe same belief.", "h2") }}

The Becoming came from a time when I needed to understand myself more deeply: my days, my energy, what was shaping my life and what I wanted to change. I needed a space to slow down, notice what was really going on and become more intentional about what came next.

She's Glowing came later, during my second pregnancy. I knew how easy it was to become completely focused on the baby and lose sight of the woman experiencing it all. I wanted something that would help me stay connected to myself, and something I wished other women had.

They are different journals for different moments.
But underneath both is the same idea: knowing yourself matters.
